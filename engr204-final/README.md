## Exam strategy (the final is 4 questions, weighted toward the end of the quarter)

Expect roughly:
- **1 transient question** — first-order **RC or RL** (find `v(t)` / `i(t)` for `t > 0`). *Very likely.*
- **1 second-order question** — **series or parallel RLC** (identify damping, write the response). *Very likely.*
- **1 DC analysis question** — nodal/mesh, or Thévenin/Norton + max power transfer.
- **1 wildcard** — AC phasor steady-state, op-amp, or AC power / power factor.

Use the **§0 "Which method do I use?" decision guide** in the cheat sheet to instantly pick an approach for any problem.
