# ENGR&204 Final Exam — Ultimate Cheat Sheet Workspace

This folder is kept **separate** from the rest of the repo (it lives only on the branch `cursor/engr204-final-cheatsheet-1465`) and is for building your Green River College ENGR&204 final cheat sheet.

## Contents
- **`CHEATSHEET.md`** — the comprehensive, unlimited-length cheat sheet. Already covers the full ENGR&204 (circuit analysis) curriculum with heavy emphasis on **RC/RL** (first-order) and **RLC** (second-order) transients, plus AC steady-state and power.
- **`source-materials/`** — ⬅️ **UPLOAD YOUR FILES HERE** (sample exams, HW, notes, quizzes, labs). See `source-materials/README.md` for one-click upload steps.

## Workflow
1. **You:** upload all your files into `source-materials/` (instructions inside that folder).
2. **You:** comment "files uploaded" in chat.
3. **Me:** read everything, solve any unsolved problems step-by-step, tailor + expand `CHEATSHEET.md` to your exact exam (matching the 4-question format and your instructor's conventions), and add worked examples.

## Printing the cheat sheet
`CHEATSHEET.md` is plain Markdown with readable formulas — open it on GitHub, or paste into Google Docs / Word and print. Since the exam allows unlimited pages, you can print as-is.
